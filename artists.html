Vincent van Gogh

Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch painter and one of the most famous artists in history. He was born in 1853 and created more than 2,000 artworks during his life, including paintings, drawings, and sketches. Van Gogh is known for his strong colors, emotional style, and expressive brushstrokes. Some of his most famous paintings include *The Starry Night*, *Sunflowers*, and *The Bedroom*. Although he was not very successful during his lifetime, his work later became extremely influential. Today, Van Gogh is remembered as a major figure in modern art, and his paintings are displayed in museums around the world.

Claude Monet

Claude Monet was a French painter and one of the founders of Impressionism. He was born in 1840 and became famous for painting light, nature, and everyday outdoor scenes. Monet often painted the same place many times in different weather and lighting conditions. Some of his most famous works include Water Lilies, Impression, Sunrise, and The Japanese Bridge. His use of soft colors and visible brushstrokes had a major influence on modern art.

Pablo Picaso

Pablo Picasso was a Spanish painter, sculptor, and one of the most important artists of the 20th century. He was born in 1881 and helped develop the art movement known as Cubism. Picasso experimented with many different styles during his long career. Some of his most famous works include Guernica, Les Demoiselles d’Avignon, and The Weeping Woman. His art changed the way people thought about shape, perspective, and representation.
